# Content collection checklist

**For:** Commerce Insight
**From:** TradyPerch
**Date:** 23 August 2026

The website is built. What it needs now is true information to put in it.

**Every item below must be verified by the institute.** Nothing on this list has
been guessed at, and nothing will be published until you confirm it. Where an
item is missing today, the website shows a neutral message rather than an
invention — so an incomplete list is not a broken website, just a quieter one.

**How to read the columns**

- **Where it goes** — you enter it yourself in the admin, or send it to us to
  put in the code.
- **Blocks** — what stays hidden or unfinished until we have it.

## 5. Results

**You enter these yourself** in *Admin → Students & Results*.

For **every** student published, we need:

| Item | Required? | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Student's full name | Yes | Stored in full; what is *shown* is controlled separately |
| Course and year | Yes | |
| Overall result (% or marks) | Yes | |
| Subject-wise marks | Optional | Now supported — "Accounts 99" reads stronger than "96%" |
| **Consent form reference** | **Yes** | How you find the signed form later |
| ☑ Permission to publish the result | **Yes** | |
| ☑ Permission to show their name | Only if showing a name | Otherwise initials are shown |
| ☑ Permission to show their photograph | Only if showing a photo | **Always a separate question** |

> ⚠ **Most of these students are under 18.** Please collect written permission
> from a parent or guardian, and keep the form. The admin will not let you
> publish without recording that you have it, and the four permissions are
> deliberately separate — agreeing to a result is not agreeing to a photograph.
>
> We can draft a consent form for your adviser to review. We should not be the
> last word on its wording.

## The short version

**Send us:** the transparent logo, a professional email address, confirmation of
your address and phone numbers, opening hours, and your Google Business Profile
link.

**Then:** course details and faculty information.

**You enter yourself, once consent forms are signed:** results, stories,
batches, announcements.

Nothing on this list will be invented. Where something is missing, the website
says so plainly and offers the visitor a way to ask you directly.
